# Helplines

Verified October 2026. Numbers change — maintainers should re-check before each release.
When the person's country isn't listed, point them to **findahelpline.com** (search by
country, free, worldwide) and their local emergency number.

## Emergency numbers (immediate danger)

| Region | Number |
|---|---|
| India | **112** |
| European Union | **112** |
| United States / Canada | **911** |
| United Kingdom | **999** (or 112) |
| Australia | **000** |

## Crisis and emotional-support lines

| Country | Service | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| India | Tele-MANAS (Govt. of India, free, 24×7, 20+ languages) | **14416** or **1-800-891-4416** |
| United States | 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline | Call or text **988** |
| United States | Crisis Text Line | Text **HOME** to **741741** |
| Canada | 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline | Call or text **988** |
| UK & Ireland | Samaritans | **116 123** (free, 24×7) |
| Australia | Lifeline | **13 11 14** |
| New Zealand | Need to Talk? | Call or text **1737** |
